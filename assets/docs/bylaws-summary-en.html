Article 1 – Name and Headquarters

The name of the Association is “Mavi ve Yeşil Ekosistem Geliştirme Derneği” (Blue and Green Ecosystem Development Association — BGEDA / MAYEKO). Its headquarters are located in Ortahisar / Trabzon, Türkiye. The Association may open branches and representative offices domestically and internationally upon decision of the General Assembly.

Article 2 – Purpose, Activities, Methodology, and Field of Operation

The Association is established to bring together the expertise and competencies of its members to generate positive contributions to social, cultural, and economic life and the natural environment through blue economy, green transition, and digital adaptation initiatives at local, regional, national, and international levels.

  • Research & Feasibility: Conducting studies, analyses, and feasibility reports on marine/water ecosystems, climate action, circular economy, industrial symbiosis, renewable energy, disaster resilience, and emerging digital technologies (AR/VR, blockchain, e-gaming, e-commerce).
  • Capacity Building & Training: Organizing seminars, workshops, conferences, and training programs on future skills, green and digital competencies, social inclusion, and entrepreneurship.
  • Grant & Project Implementation: Designing and executing funded projects under European Union programs (Horizon Europe, Erasmus+, Interreg NEXT Black Sea Basin), development agencies, and international grant-making bodies.
  • Knowledge Platforms: Establishing open digital and physical libraries, publications, and shared collaborative spaces for researchers, practitioners, and society.
  • Multi-Stakeholder Partnerships: Building joint networks and clusters with universities, public authorities, municipalities, and civil society organizations.

Articles 3 – 5 – Membership Rights and Procedures

Any natural or legal person possessing legal capacity who embraces the aims and principles of the Association may apply for membership. Written applications are decided by the Board of Directors within 30 days. Individuals or institutions providing outstanding contributions may be granted Honorary Membership.

Articles 6 – 12 – Governing Bodies

  • General Assembly (Art. 7–9): The supreme decision-making body composed of registered full members. Ordinary sessions convene every 3 years in February.
  • Board of Directors (Art. 10): Composed of 5 principal and 5 alternate members elected by secret ballot to represent and manage the Association.
  • Supervisory Board (Art. 11): Composed of 3 principal and 3 alternate members auditing financial and administrative operations at intervals not exceeding one year.

Articles 13 – 27 – Financial Provisions, Internal Audit, and Amendments

  • Revenue Sources (Art. 13): Membership fees, national and international project grants, donations, publications/events, and economic enterprise revenues.
  • Transparency & Audit (Art. 19–20): Subject to regular internal audits by the Supervisory Board as well as independent external project audits required by international donors.
